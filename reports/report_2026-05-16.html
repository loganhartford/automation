Spintly is a Goa-based IoT startup that builds a fully wireless, cloud-managed physical access control system for commercial buildings. Its hardware product range includes Bluetooth and NFC-enabled access readers, QR-code scanners, and biometric devices such as facial recognition and fingerprint systems. The company combines this wireless hardware — including access readers, controllers, gateways, and smart locks — with a recurring revenue model driven by its cloud platform and remote management tools. Its software suite offers cloud-based modules for access management, attendance tracking, visitor management, and video surveillance with AI-driven analytics. The core technical differentiator is how the hardware communicates. The startup's patented wireless mesh architecture — protected by seven patents — enables secure connectivity and mobile credential access using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) mesh networking. Unlike traditional systems, Spintly uses a distributed IoT architecture and edge computing technology, which eliminates the need for heavy back-end infrastructure and enables smartphone-based door access to users. It also supports secure access management and local log storage during power outages, ensuring operational continuity. The problem Spintly is solving is the cost and complexity of legacy wired access control. The technology replaces complex wired systems, reducing installation time, deployment costs, and operational complexity while enabling remote management through cloud-based tools. Spintly is targeting enterprise customers managing access across large commercial spaces and multi-location facilities. The wireless architecture makes the solution particularly attractive to corporate offices, co-working spaces, tech parks, and large enterprises that prioritize scalable, low-maintenance security systems. On founding and funding: Spintly was founded in 2018 by Rohin Parkar and Malcolm D'Souza. Parkar brings over 18 years of experience in wireless technology and is a member of the Thread Group and Bluetooth SIG, driving adoption and standardization of wireless mesh for access control and building automation. CTO Malcolm D'Souza has 18 years of experience having worked with Motorola and Nokia in Chicago, where he was deeply involved in the development of femto-cell and liquid cloud radio technology. The company has raised capital across several rounds: in 2022, Spintly secured $1.4 million in a Pre-Series A round with participation from LetsVenture, Accel Nest, and others, followed by a $2 million extended seed round from Accel, Chakra Growth Fund, and others. Most recently, Spintly raised $8 million in a Series A round led by Accel, with participation from Enrission India Capital, SucSEED Ventures, Alumni Ventures, and Chakra Growth Fund. Total raised across all rounds amounts to roughly $11–12 million. On traction: Spintly has eliminated 200,000 plastic badges and 2,000 miles of wired infrastructure from the built world, and currently serves 300+ customers and 4,000+ doors. The firm operates across India, the Middle East and Africa, and the United States. Competitors include Openpath, Brivo, Proxy, Doordec, and Nexkey. With the Series A proceeds, 60% of the capital will support sales and marketing expansion across India, MEA, and the US, while 20% will be allocated to R&D for AI-driven smart building capabilities.
